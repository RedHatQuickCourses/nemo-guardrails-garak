= Lab Environment

== Instructions to Launch Your Lab on the Red Hat Demo Platform (RHDP)

IMPORTANT: In case you don't have access to lab offered by Red Hat Demo Platform (RHDP) then you can bring your own lab environment with following requirement:

. OpenShift Version - 4.21
. Cluster Size - multinode
. OpenShift Worker Count - 2
. OpenShift Worker CPU - 16
. OpenShift Worker Memory - 64Gi

=== Red Hat Associates

. **Log in** to the https://demo.redhat.com/[RHDP portal,window=_blank]
. **Search** for the catalog entry: **Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform Cluster (Multi-Cloud)**.
. **Click** on the catalog entry in the search results.
. On the catalog page, **click** the **Order** button.
. **Fill out** the required details in the order form.
.. Cloud Provider - cnv
.. OpenShift Version - 4.21
.. Cluster Size - multinode
.. OpenShift Worker Count - 2
.. OpenShift Worker CPU - 16
.. OpenShift Worker Memory - 64Gi
. **Review the warning** at the bottom of the form and **check the box** labeled: +
   *“I confirm that I understand the above warnings.”*
. **Click** the **Order** button to place your lab order.

=== Red Hat Partners

. **Log in** to the https://partner.demo.redhat.com/[RHDP partner portal,window=_blank]
. **Search** for the catalog entry: **Red Hat OpenShift Container Platform Cluster**.
. **Click** on the catalog entry in the search results.
. On the catalog page, **click** the **Order** button.
. **Fill out** the required details in the order form.
.. OpenShift worker memory size - 64Gi
.. OpenShift Version - 4.20
. **Review the warning** at the bottom of the form and **check the box** labeled: +
   *“I confirm that I understand the above warnings.”*
. **Click** the **Order** button to place your lab order.

=== Important Notes:
- This lab may take approximately **90 minutes** to become ready.
- You will receive an **email with access details** once your lab environment is ready.
- You can also **retrieve lab access** directly from the RHDP portal.

=== How to Access Your Lab via the RHDP Portal:
. On the RHDP portal, **click** on the **Services** option in the left-hand menu.
. **Select your lab** from the listings on the right-hand side of the page to view access details.
